Objective

The objective of this lab is to understand how Generative AI can create platform-specific aviation marketing content while maintaining factual consistency and compliance.

The lab focuses on adapting marketing copy for different platforms, avoiding unsupported promotional claims, checking facts, reviewing language and ensuring human approval before publication.

Platform-Specific Marketing Copy

The same marketing brief should not be copied identically across every platform because different platforms have different purposes, audiences and communication styles.

Platform	Main Purpose	Typical Style
LinkedIn	Professional communication	Informative and credible
Instagram	Visual engagement	Short and audience-friendly
Advertisement Headline	Capture attention	Concise and memorable
Promotional Email	Explain an offer	Structured and action-oriented
Website	Provide detailed information	Accurate and easy to navigate
Important Principle

The format and tone should be adapted to the platform while keeping the underlying facts consistent.

Discount: Up to 10% on the base fare
Booking Period: 1–15 October
Travel Period: 15 October–15 December

Booking Channel: Official Aero Vista website and mobile application
Applicable Conditions

Baggage, taxes, cancellation and change conditions are subject to applicable fare rules.

Unsupported Claims

The campaign brief does not support claims that:

Every student receives a 10% discount.
The offer applies to all domestic routes.
Baggage is free.
Tickets are refundable.
There are no additional charges.
The offer is the cheapest available fare.
Seats are guaranteed.

These claims must not be added to AI-generated marketing content.

Master Marketing Prompt

Act as a marketing communications executive for the fictional airline AeroVista. Using only the verified campaign brief provided, create platform-specific promotional content for the Aero Vista Student Saver campaign. The target audience is college and university students. Do not claim that the offer applies to every route, that all students will receive the maximum discount, that baggage is free, that tickets are refundable or that seats are guaranteed. Clearly mention that the offer applies to selected domestic routes, requires valid student identification and is subject to availability and applicable fare rules. Do not invent additional benefits, prices or policy conditions.

Non-Compliant Marketing Copy

Students, your dream trip is finally here! AeroVista guarantees every student a 10% discount on every domestic flight. Enjoy free baggage, completely refundable tickets and no additional charges. Seats are unlimited, so book whenever you want. AeroVista offers India’s cheapest student flights.

Problems Identified
Claim	Supported?	Correction
Every student receives 10%	No	Use “eligible students can receive up to 10%”
Every domestic flight	No	State “selected domestic routes”
Free baggage	No	Remove claim
Completely refundable tickets	No	Refer to applicable fare rules
No additional charges	No	Remove claim
Unlimited seats	No	State seats are limited and subject to availability
Cheapest student flights	No	Remove unless supported by verified evidence

Fact-Difference Check

A fact-difference check compares the original verified information with AI-generated content to identify missing, changed or incorrect facts.

Examples
“Up to 10%” → “10% guaranteed” = Changed
Selected domestic routes → Every domestic flight = Changed
Student ID required → Missing = Missing information
Booking dates → Missing = Missing information
Travel dates → Missing = Missing information
Limited availability → Unlimited seats = Changed
Applicable fare rules → Free baggage/refundable tickets = Changed
Official website and mobile application → Website only = Missing information
Register Check

A register check examines whether marketing language is appropriate for the intended audience and situation.

Examples

Casual/Exaggerated:

Grab this insane deal now!

Professional Rewrite:

Explore the Aero Vista Student Saver offer.

Misleading:

Everyone gets the full discount!

Professional and Accurate Rewrite:

Eligible students can receive up to 10% off the base fare.

Exaggerated/Misleading:

The cheapest flight offer ever!

This claim should be removed unless supported by verified evidence.

Marketing Compliance Checklist

Before publication, the following checks should be completed:

Every factual claim is supported by the campaign brief.

Dates and figures are correct.

Conditions are clearly stated.

Absolute claims are avoided.

Guaranteed benefits are avoided unless verified.

Content is suitable for the target audience.

Tone is appropriate for each platform.

Brand language is consistent.

Sensitive details are excluded.

Call to action is accurate.

Human approval is completed before publication.

Key Learnings
Marketing content should be adapted to the purpose and audience of each platform.
Underlying facts must remain consistent across platforms.
“Up to 10%” is different from “10% guaranteed.”
Promotional claims must not go beyond verified facts.
Important offer conditions should always be included.
Fact-difference checks identify changed, missing or incorrect information.
Register checks identify casual, exaggerated, professional or misleading language.
Unsupported claims such as guaranteed employment or cheapest prices should be avoided.
Compliance checklists help verify facts, dates, conditions, tone and calls to action.
Peer review provides an additional quality and compliance check.
AI-generated marketing content requires human approval before publication.
Conclusion

This lab demonstrated how Generative AI can assist in creating aviation marketing content for different platforms while maintaining factual accuracy and compliance.

The activities showed the importance of adapting tone and format without changing verified facts, identifying misleading claims, performing fact-difference and register checks, and clearly communicating important conditions.

Human review remains essential before AI-generated marketing content is published because AI may generate exaggerated, inaccurate or unsupported claims.
